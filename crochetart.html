The Maker

Hi, I'm Maria.

Every bag and bouquet on this site started as loose yarn on my kitchen table in Windsor. I taught myself to crochet flowers that could actually pass for the real thing — and bags sturdy enough for the everyday chaos of a diaper bag, a grocery run, or a night out.

Nothing here is mass-produced. Each order is stitched by hand, one loop at a time, which is exactly why it takes a little patience — and exactly why it's worth it.

— Maria, Magic Stitch
